Nontendo Sports

Tap start, then hold your phone still for a second.

🎳 Bowling
  1. Hold it like a TV remote: screen facing up, top of the phone pointing at the laptop.
  2. Tilt left or right to aim. Watch the line on the big screen.
  3. Press and hold the big button with your thumb.
  4. Swing your arm back, then forward like a real bowl.
  5. Let go of the button as your arm comes forward.
  6. For a curve: twist your wrist as you let go.
⛳ Golf
  1. Hold it like a club grip with both hands: top of the phone pointing at the floor, screen facing you.
  2. Stand side-on like a golfer. Use the ◀ ▶ buttons to aim and change club.
  3. Press and hold the screen with your thumbs and keep holding.
  4. Swing back: the further back, the more power. Watch the meter on the big screen.
  5. Swing through: the ball is hit automatically as the phone passes the start point.
  6. Keep the phone face square to hit straight. Twisting it at impact hooks or slices.
